On To The Next — Pilot Terms of Use

Effective September 24, 2026

On To The Next is an owner-operated itinerary-planning pilot. The planning application runs locally; these public pages describe the pilot and do not provide access to its application or database. The project owner operates the pilot and controls access.

Recommendations and saved plans help organize a trip. They do not confirm opening on a travel date, ticket or event availability, accessibility, travel time, or the feasibility of an itinerary. Check important details with the relevant venue or organizer before relying on them. The application does not book travel, take payment, or enter into a booking transaction for you.

Optional Google place matching, current context, reviews, and one-photo requests occur only when the owner activates Google and explicitly requests them. Independent recommendations and the Add action remain available without requesting Google content. A linked place does not verify every part of an app-authored walk or experience.

Use of Google Maps content is subject to the Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service and the applicable Google Terms of Service. Those terms are incorporated for the Google portions of this pilot. Keep Google Maps, provider, and contributor attributions and source links visible. Do not use this pilot to bulk download, archive, redistribute, or train an AI model on Google content.

Reviews are a provider-selected sample in relevance order, not a complete survey of travelers. Photos may not show current conditions or the precise experience being planned. Hours supplied as current or regular hours are not confirmation for future trip dates.

This Google-only pilot does not operate live Brave search, OpenAI extraction, or Viator integration. The owner controls the limited evaluation and may disable Google at any time. Saved independent plans and persistent usage accounting are retained; restarting the app does not replenish its allowance.

The Pilot Privacy Notice explains data handling. For questions about this owner-operated pilot, use your existing private communication channel with the project owner. This pilot does not offer a public customer-service or booking service.
